Pursuit: Cold Trail

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

The short version: Pursuit: Cold Trail does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. The only transaction is the optional one-time purchase, which Apple or Google processes and RevenueCat verifies using an anonymous ID.

Who we are

Pursuit: Cold Trail (the "App") is a geography detective game made by an independent developer, The Tan Boys ("we," "us," or "our"). This policy explains how the App handles information. It covers the App on the Apple App Store and Google Play.

Information we collect

None. The App does not collect, receive, or store personal information from anyone who plays it, including:

  • names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other contact details
  • precise or approximate location
  • photos, contacts, microphone, or camera data
  • advertising IDs or other tracking identifiers
  • usage data, analytics, or crash reports

The App has no sign-up or login, and players cannot chat with each other or post anything.

Game progress

Your progress, such as which cases you've solved, is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the App deletes this progress.

Purchases

The first case is free. You can unlock the other cases with a single in-app purchase. Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) processes the purchase entirely through your store account. We never see or receive your name, payment details, or billing information. To confirm your unlock, purchases are verified through RevenueCat, a service that receives an anonymous app ID and purchase receipt information. RevenueCat does not receive your name, email address, or location, and we do not use this information for advertising or tracking. This lets the App open the extra cases or restore them on a new device. The privacy policies of Apple, Google, and RevenueCat cover how they handle purchases:

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy

Advertising and third parties

The App has no advertising and no third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising tools. The only third-party service is RevenueCat, used solely to verify the in-app purchase as described above. We do not sell or rent information to anyone.

Children's privacy

The App is designed for kids and families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or from anyone else. This is consistent with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and app store requirements for children's apps. Because the App collects nothing, it has no children's data to review, correct, or delete. Parents with questions can contact us using the details below.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws may give you rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information. Since the App collects none, we hold nothing to provide or delete. You can still contact us with any request or question.

Changes to this policy

If the App ever changes how it handles information, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect. If a change would involve collecting information from children, we will get any parental consent the law requires first.
